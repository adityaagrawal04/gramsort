# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community

## Responsibilities
Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.
